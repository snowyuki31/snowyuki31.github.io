import { Definition, Example } from '@components/env';

<Definition title="関手" id="def-functor">
[[圏]] $\mathcal{C}$ から圏 $\mathcal{D}$ への**関手**（functor）$F$ とは、次を満たす対応をいう。

- $\mathcal{C}$ の各対象 $A$ を $\mathcal{D}$ の対象 $F(A)$ に移す
- $\mathcal{C}$ の各射 $f \colon A \to B$ を $\mathcal{D}$ の射 $F(f) \colon F(A) \to F(B)$ に移す
- 恒等射を保つ: $F(\mathrm{id}_A) = \mathrm{id}_{F(A)}$
- 合成を保つ: $F(g \circ f) = F(g) \circ F(f)$
</Definition>

```tikz
\usepackage{tikz}
\usetikzlibrary{arrows.meta}
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[
  every node/.style = {font=\small},
  obj/.style   = {draw, circle, minimum size=9mm, inner sep=0pt},
  hom/.style   = {->, shorten >=2pt, shorten <=2pt},
  idloop/.style = {->, loop, min distance=8mm, looseness=6},
  fmap/.style  = {->, dashed, gray!70, shorten >=2pt, shorten <=2pt},
]

% ===== 圏 C（左）=====
\node[obj] (A) at (0,4)  {$A$};
\node[obj] (B) at (0,2)  {$B$};
\node[obj] (C) at (0,0)  {$C$};

\draw[hom] (A) -- node[left]  {$f$} (B);
\draw[hom] (B) -- node[left]  {$g$} (C);
\draw[hom, bend right=70] (A) to (C);
\node at (-2.3,2) {\scriptsize $g\circ f$};
\draw[idloop] (A) edge[in=150,out=90] node[above] {$\mathrm{id}_A$} (A);

\node at (0,5) {$\mathcal{C}$};

% ===== 圏 D（右）=====
\node[obj] (FA) at (7,4)  {$F(A)$};
\node[obj] (FB) at (7,2)  {$F(B)$};
\node[obj] (FC) at (7,0)  {$F(C)$};

\draw[hom] (FA) -- node[right] {$F(f)$} (FB);
\draw[hom] (FB) -- node[right] {$F(g)$} (FC);
\draw[hom, bend left=70] (FA) to (FC);
\node at (9.3,2) {\scriptsize $F(g)\!\circ\! F(f)$};
\draw[idloop] (FA) edge[in=90,out=30] node[above] {$\mathrm{id}_{F(A)}$} (FA);

\node at (7,5) {$\mathcal{D}$};

% ===== 関手 F の対応（点線矢印）=====
\draw[fmap] (A) -- (FA);
\draw[fmap] (B) -- (FB);
\draw[fmap] (C) -- (FC);
\node[gray!70] at (3.5,3.3) {\scriptsize $F$};

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

関手は、圏の構造（恒等射と合成）を壊さずに一方の圏を他方の圏へ写し取る対応である。
すでに見た「構造を保つ写像」はどれも関手の特別な場合になっている:

- モノイド準同型は、対象が 1 つの圏（[[モノイド]]）どうしを結ぶ関手（[[モノイドの圏]]）
- 順序を保つ写像（単調写像）は、[[半順序集合]]どうしを結ぶ関手

全く異なる分野に現れる「準同型的なもの」を、関手という 1 つの概念でまとめて扱える。

<Example title="忘却関手" id="ex-forgetful">
[[モノイドの圏]] $\mathbf{Mon}$ から集合の圏 $\mathbf{Set}$ への関手として、
モノイド $(M, \cdot, 1_M)$ から演算を忘れて台集合 $M$ だけを残す
**忘却関手**（forgetful functor）$U$ が考えられる。
</Example>

```tikz
\usepackage{amssymb}
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
% left box: Mon
\node at (1.7,4.0) {$\mathbf{Mon}$};
\draw[rounded corners, gray] (-0.7,-1.5) rectangle (4.1,3.6);
\node (MN) at (1.7,2.6) {$(\mathbb{N},+)$};
\node (MZ) at (1.7,0.0) {$(\mathbb{Z},+)$};
\draw[->] (MN) -- node[left] {$f: n \mapsto 3n$} (MZ);
\draw[->] (MN) edge[loop above] node[above] {$\mathrm{id}$} (MN);
% right box: Set
\node at (9.3,4.0) {$\mathbf{Set}$};
\draw[rounded corners, gray] (6.9,-1.5) rectangle (11.7,3.6);
\node (SN) at (9.3,2.6) {$\mathbb{N}$};
\node (SZ) at (9.3,0.0) {$\mathbb{Z}$};
\draw[->] (SN) -- node[right] {$U(f): n \mapsto 3n$} (SZ);
\draw[->] (SN) edge[loop above] node[above] {$\mathrm{id}$} (SN);
% the functor
\draw[->, very thick] (4.5,3.9) -- node[above] {$U$ (forgetful)} (6.5,3.9);
% object and morphism assignments
\draw[|->, dashed, gray] (MN) -- node[above] {drop $+$, keep the set} (SN);
\draw[|->, dashed, gray] (MZ) -- (SZ);
% functor laws
\node at (5.5,-2.3) {$U(\mathrm{id}_M) = \mathrm{id}_{U(M)},\qquad U(g \circ f) = U(g) \circ U(f)$};
\node at (5.5,-3.0) {same map $n \mapsto 3n$, seen without the monoid structure};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

<Example title="自由関手" id="ex-free">
逆に、集合 $A$ から「$A$ の文字列と連接のモノイド」$A^{*}$ を作る対応は
$\mathbf{Set}$ から $\mathbf{Mon}$ への関手になり、**自由関手**（free functor）$F$ と呼ぶ。
</Example>

```tikz
\usepackage{amssymb}
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
% left box: Set
\node at (1.5,4.0) {$\mathbf{Set}$};
\draw[rounded corners, gray] (-0.9,-1.5) rectangle (3.9,3.6);
\node (SX) at (1.5,2.6) {$\{x\}$};
\node (SA) at (1.5,0.0) {$\{a,b\}$};
\draw[->] (SX) -- node[left] {$g: x \mapsto a$} (SA);
% right box: Mon
\node at (9.3,4.0) {$\mathbf{Mon}$};
\draw[rounded corners, gray] (6.3,-1.5) rectangle (12.3,3.6);
\node (MX) at (9.3,2.6) {$\{x\}^* = \{\varepsilon, x, xx, \ldots\}$};
\node (MA) at (9.3,0.0) {$\{a,b\}^* = \{\varepsilon, a, b, ab, ba, \ldots\}$};
\draw[->] (MX) -- node[right] {$F(g): xx \mapsto aa$} (MA);
% the functor
\draw[->, very thick] (4.3,3.9) -- node[above] {$F$ (free)} (6.1,3.9);
% object assignments
\draw[|->, dashed, gray] (SX) -- node[above] {words + concatenation} (MX);
\draw[|->, dashed, gray] (SA) -- (MA);
% notes
\node at (5.7,-2.3) {$\varepsilon$ = empty word (unit), $\{x\}^* \cong (\mathbb{N},+)$ via $x^n \leftrightarrow n$};
\node at (5.7,-3.0) {$F(g)$ replaces each letter by its image: no relations to check};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

忘却関手 $U$ と自由関手 $F$ が対をなしていることは、先へ進むと随伴という概念につながる。
